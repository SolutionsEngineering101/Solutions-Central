## Brief
LTTS wants custom email to be sent to the manager of the employees celebrating milestone (5,10,15.. ) 1 week in advance.

## Problem Description
LTTS wants custom email to be sent to the reporting manager of the employees celebrating milestone (5,10,15.. ) 1 week in advance. 

This is a requirement from there CHRO so want this to be implemented at the earliest, they want this to be active by 25th Aug.

## Solution Given
Email configuration
We can configure the email using the content provided by LTTS, with their existing ROAR branding. 
Email trigger
The email can be triggered to the reporting manager 7 days before the milestone date, with one email sent per eligible employee. The trigger will apply only to configured milestone years, such as 5, 10, 15 years, and so on, rather than every annual work anniversary.
