# Solution Request #326 - Reliance Brands Limited
**Date:** 2026-09-16  |  **Status:** Solution Given Closed  |  **Complexity:** Not Set

## Subject
Solution Request : 326326 : Reliance Brands Limited - Platform Access Inquiry

## Brief
Hi Team,

We are in advanced discussions with Reliance Brands Limited, and one of their key requirements is around platform access for retail store employees who will log in using Employee IDs, as most of them will not have corporate email IDs.

The client wants to ensure that an employee who exits the organization cannot continue accessing the platform, without having to rely entirely on the HR team uploading a CSV file to deactivate access.

For employees with email IDs, they have requested OTP-based login, which gives us an additional layer of authentication. However, for store employees using Employee ID + registered mobile number, OTP-based login alone may not prevent access after exit if their access status has not yet been updated in the platform.

Could you please advise:

1. Is there any existing configuration/workflow that can automatically restrict access for Employee ID-based users after exit?
2. Can we introduce an additional validation at login to ensure the employee is still active?
3. If not, are there any alternative authentication or integration-based approaches we can propose? Specifically, is there a way to minimize the access gap between an employee's exit and the HR team's next CSV/delta upload?

The client's primary concern is avoiding any window where exited employees could continue accessing the platform due to a delay in updating the employee file.

Requesting your guidance on what is technically feasible and the best workaround we can propose to the client.

## Problem Description
Hi Team,

We are in advanced discussions with Reliance Brands Limited, and one of their key requirements is around platform access for retail store employees who will log in using Employee IDs, as most of them will not have corporate email IDs.

Could you please advise:

1. Is there any existing configuration/workflow that can automatically restrict access for Employee ID-based users after exit?
2. Can we introduce an additional validation at login to ensure the employee is still active?
3. If not, are there any alternative authentication or integration-based approaches we can propose? Specifically, is there a way to minimize the access gap between an employee's exit and the HR team's next CSV/delta upload?

The client's primary concern is avoiding any window where exited employees could continue accessing the platform due to a delay in updating the employee file.

Requesting your guidance on what is technically feasible and the best workaround we can propose to the client.

## Solution Given
Automated Access Revocation (Date of Leaving)
To solve the client’s core concern regarding former employees retaining access, we will utilize the Date of Leaving (DOL) field.
How It Works: Reliance Brands simply includes the "Date of Leaving" column in their regular employee data file. HR populates this as soon as a resignation is accepted, no manual action is needed on the employee's actual last day.
Automated Job Execution: A daily system background job checks these dates. Any employee whose leaving date has passed will have their account, active sessions, and access revoked automatically.
Key Timings:
Future Dates: Safe to enter early. The system takes action only after the date passes.
3. Critical Data Dependency for the Client
It is essential to emphasize to the client that our platform relies entirely on their data feed to detect an exit.
Data Drives Access: An exited employee passes any login gate (Email or SMS OTP) as long as their platform record remains marked active. Access restriction relies on HR sharing the data, not the login method itself.
Process Risk Warning: If the client's current process involves simply removing/dropping former employees from the file instead of marking them inactive or including a DOL, those accounts will remain active indefinitely. We must ensure they explicitly send leaver records.
4. Recommended File Sync Cadence: Weekly
We recommend Reliance Brands send employee data on a weekly cycle.
Because DOL is forward-dated weeks in advance, a weekly file captured during the notice period gives the platform plenty of time to queue and auto-revoke access on the exact date.
Next Steps & Required Confirmations
Confirm that all retail store employees are based in India.
Confirm that mobile numbers are accurately captured in their HR system and shared with us in the employee details file.
