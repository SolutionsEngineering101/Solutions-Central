# Solution Request #310 - Parekh Integrated Services Private Limited
**Date:** 2026-08-27  |  **Status:** Open  |  **Complexity:** Not Set

## Brief
Feasibility to trigger mobile notifications for Announcement created on platform

## Problem Description
We need to assess the feasibility of triggering mobile notifications for Announcements created on the RnR platform, including WhatsApp, SMS, and push notifications for users who have the VC mobile app installed.

This requirement is particularly relevant for clients where more than 50% of the workforce comprises blue-collar employees without corporate email IDs. In such cases, platform admins need alternative channels to ensure that important organisational announcements reach all employees.

Since these announcements may contain critical information that needs to be communicated to the entire workforce, we would like to explore the feasibility of supporting email along with WhatsApp, SMS, and/or mobile push notifications, wherever technically possible, to ensure maximum reach and timely communication.
