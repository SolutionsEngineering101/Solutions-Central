## Subject

## Brief
Kotak LSA

## Problem Description
For Kotak LSA Opportunity, we proposed a Milestone Catalogue-based LSA solution that gives employees the flexibility to choose between Salary Credit, Gift Cards, or Merchandise instead of the current salary-only model. The catalogue would open 30 days before the service anniversary, with automated reminders to encourage timely selection. Employees opting for Salary Credit would still receive their milestone plaque/certificate through a separate fulfilment process, while payroll payout would be handled by Kotak. A fallback mechanism was also suggested where non-responding employees can be routed to Salary Credit after the redemption window closes.
