# Solution Request #330 - Ultratech Wires & Cables
**Date:** 2026-09-17  |  **Status:** Unknown  |  **Complexity:** Not Set

## Brief
We have received a requirement from UltraTech Wires & Cables to introduce separate timelines for the nomination and approval stages of their Standard Awards. Specifically, they would like to configure individual timelines for:

Nomination submission
First-level approval
Second-level approval

The client has explained that these are monthly awards, and nominations submitted for a particular month should be completed within a particular period. They should not remain pending or carry forward into subsequent months.

As an alternative, we suggested using Panel Awards. However, although the client is already familiar with the Panel Awards process, they do not wish to introduce panelists for these monthly awards. Therefore, Panel Awards may not be a suitable option for this requirement.

Could you please advise whether the requested timelines can be configured for Standard Awards? If this is not feasible, please suggest the best possible workaround that we can propose to the client.

One potential workaround we considered is to configure the process so that:
Any pending nominations are automatically declined at a specified date.
New nominations are closed after a defined cut-off date.
Nominations are reopened on a specific date for the next monthly cycle.
Any pending first- or second-level approvals are automatically declined after the monthly deadline.

The approval process is reopened or made available for the next cycle based on the configured schedule.
Please review this requirement and let us know whether such an approach can be implemented. If not, kindly recommend the most suitable alternative.
