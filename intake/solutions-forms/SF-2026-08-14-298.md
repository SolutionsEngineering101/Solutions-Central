# Solution Request #298 - MBRDI
**Date:** 2026-08-14  |  **Status:** Unknown  |  **Complexity:** Not Set

## Brief
MBRDI_New requirement for automated report and Custom report for Campaigns

## Problem Description
We have 2 new requests from Mercedes-Benz that need your support.
1. Automation of Department HR Access Report
For Mercedes-Benz, budget deductions happen through their department wallets. To enable this, each department wallet is created as a unique user on our backend, and these are reflected in their Applause dashboard as well.
On their dashboard, under the department section, various HRs are mapped against each department. Since these department-HR mappings are updated frequently, we currently share a manual access list/report with the MBRDI team each month. This report is compiled manually — David from the RnD team shares the base data, and Akhlasur then formats it as per the client's requirement.
This process is time-consuming and entirely manual. We'd like your support to automate this report on a monthly basis.
I'm attaching the current report format that's shared with them every month, for reference.
2. Bifurcation of Campaign Badges in Appreciation Report
The client has requested that badges issued via Campaign Manager be clearly labelled as "campaign badges" in the appreciation report. Currently, these appear as regular badges, making it difficult for the client to distinguish them from standard/permanent badges.
We're looking at two possible approaches:
A separate report specifically for campaign manager badges, or
Including them within the existing appreciation report, with a clear bifurcation/label to differentiate them from regular badges.
