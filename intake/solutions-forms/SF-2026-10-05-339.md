## Subject
Solution Request : 339339 : AM Green Secondary Wallet - Diwali Gifting Checkout Address

## Brief
Action Item – Secondary Wallet-Diwali Gifting-Checkout Address:
Disabling the “Edit” Option

## Problem Description
Action Item – Secondary Wallet-Diwali Gifting-Checkout Address:
Disabling the “Edit” Option:
The feasibility of disabling the “Edit” option for the delivery address at the checkout stage needs to be checked.
This requirement is based on AM Green’s fulfilment process, wherein the ordered items will be dispatched to designated office locations across four different locations, including Mumbai and Hyderabad. The objective is to ensure that deliveries are routed directly to the respective office premises, allowing employees to receive/collect their selected gifts from their office locations without any hassle or delay.

## Solution Given
The office addresses will be on saved address until Diwali Gifting is over for both Primary and Secondary wallet and once this activity is done, the configuration will be rolled back
