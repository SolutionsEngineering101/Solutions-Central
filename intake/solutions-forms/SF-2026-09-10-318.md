## Problem Description
Hi Team,

We are currently in discussions with Tata Starbucks for a redemption platform (~5,300 employees PAN India). Their HR Head has raised the below questions, and we need clarity on the platform capabilities:

1. Points Upload & Redemption Reports: Can the columns in these reports be customized—i.e., existing columns modified or additional columns added as required?

2. Scheduled Reports: Can reports be scheduled to be automatically sent to specific POCs at defined intervals?

3. Points Expiry: Can expiry be configured at the time of points allocation via CSV, based on their preferred frequency (monthly/quarterly/yearly)?

For example, with a monthly expiry:
Points allocated in September remain valid until October and then expire if unused.
A fresh allocation in October would have its own expiry in November.
The points statement should clearly reflect each allocation, its expiry date, and the corresponding utilization/expiry status.

Can the system support this level of expiry configuration and tracking?

4. Unutilized Points Report: Is there a report that can provide visibility into points that remain unutilized/unused by employees?

Could you please confirm what is currently supported, along with any limitations or workarounds, so we can respond accurately to the client?

## Solution Given
1. Points Upload & Redemption Reports
Points Upload: The Points Upload CSV can be customized based on the required fields/columns.
Redemption Report: The Redemption Report columns can be customized. Any additional columns or changes to the existing redemption report structure would require a custom development and provided the data is provided during employee data integration
2. Scheduled Reports
Yes, reports can be scheduled and automatically shared with specific POCs at defined intervals, based on the available scheduling options.
3. Points Expiry
Currently, points expiry is configured at the company level and cannot be defined separately at the time of each points allocation through CSV.
For example, if a monthly expiry is required where September allocations expire in October and October allocations expire in November, the system would need to support allocation/batch-level expiry. This is not currently supported through the CSV allocation process.
Additionally, users do not currently have visibility into allocation-wise points expiry and utilization. As a VC Admin, the expiry configuration can be managed at the company level.
The requested functionality where each allocation has its own expiry date and the points statement reflects the allocation, expiry date, utilization, and expiry status would therefore require an enhancement to the existing functionality.
4. Unutilized Points Report
The availability of an employee-level report specifically showing unutilized/unused points would need to be validated based on the existing reporting capabilities. If the required allocation, redemption, and expiry-level data is available, a customized report may be considered to provide visibility into the unutilized points.
