## Problem Description
For the company store: What if as HR I wanted to recognize someone, but I wanted the employee to specifically get a particular item from the company store, is this possible or not?

Merchandise - almost same question with the company store. What if I wanted to send a particular item or gift set to all Philippine employees, is it possible for me as HR to send the points to employees but the item is pre-selected for them?

## Solution Given
For both scenarios, we would recommend leveraging Manager Gifting as the most suitable approach:
1. Recognizing an individual employee with a specific item
If HR wants to recognize an employee and ensure they receive a specific item from the Company Store, Manager Gifting can be used to directly gift the employee the selected merchandise/gift, rather than having the employee choose an item themselves.
2. Sending a specific gift/merchandise to a group of employees
For example, if HR wants to send the same pre-selected gift set or merchandise item to all employees in the Philippines, Manager Gifting can be used to distribute the selected gift directly to the intended employees. This provides HR with greater control over the gifting experience and ensures consistency across the group. For this, we need to reach out to Merchandise team who will help you out in configuring the merchandise available for Philippines.
