# Solution Request #327 - BDO Rise
**Date:** 2026-09-17  |  **Status:** Unknown  |  **Complexity:** Not Set

## Brief
Advance Scheduling of Monetary Award Certificates

## Problem Description
I would like to propose an enhancement that could significantly improve the way we manage monetary award certificates on the platform.

Current Challenge

In some cases, clients share multiple certificates with us at the beginning of the year—for example, four certificates corresponding to awards for each quarter. Currently, the CSM has to remember when each certificate needs to be made live and manually configure it at the appropriate time.

This creates a dependency on manual follow-ups and increases the possibility of a certificate being missed or activated later than intended.

Proposed Enhancement

Introduce the ability to schedule monetary award certificates in advance.

For example:

Client shares 4 certificates at the beginning of the year.
CSM configures all 4 certificates at once.
For each certificate, the CSM selects its go-live/activation date.
The platform automatically makes the respective certificate available on the scheduled date.

Example - 

| Certificate   | Award Period | Scheduled Go-Live |
| ------------- | ------------ | ----------------- |
| Certificate 1 | Q1           | April 1           |
| Certificate 2 | Q2           | July 1            |
| Certificate 3 | Q3           | October 1         |
| Certificate 4 | Q4           | January 1         |


Business Value

This would make the process much more automated and reliable. Once all certificates are configured at the beginning of the year, the CSM would not have to remember that he/she has to make a certificate live.

It would also:

Reduce manual intervention for CSMs.
Minimise the chances of missing a scheduled certificate.
Provide a more predictable experience for clients.
Allow CSMs to complete the entire year's certificate configuration upfront.
Reduce operational dependency on individual CSM reminders and follow-ups.

In short, configure once and let the platform take care of the rest. This could be a simple enhancement with a meaningful impact on operational efficiency and certificate management.

Would be great if the Product team could evaluate the feasibility of this enhancement.
