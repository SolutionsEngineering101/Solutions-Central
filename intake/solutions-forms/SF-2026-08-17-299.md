# Solution Request #299 - Syrma SGS Technology
**Date:** 2026-08-17  |  **Status:** Unknown  |  **Complexity:** Not Set

## Brief
WhatsApp capabilities, voucher notifications, and phone-based onboarding for employees without email IDs.

## Problem Description
One of our prospects, Syrma SGS Technology, has approximately 2,000 employees, including a significant blue-collar workforce. They are evaluating our WhatsApp notification capabilities, particularly to improve communication and accessibility for employees who may not regularly use email or the platform.

They would like clarity on the following:

Complete list of WhatsApp capabilities - Please share the complete list of notifications, updates, and other functionalities that can currently be delivered or supported through WhatsApp.

Gift Voucher Notifications - The prospect specifically wants to understand whether the following can be communicated through WhatsApp:
Gift voucher details when an employee redeems their points.
Gift voucher details when vouchers are directly issued/sent to employees by the HR/Admin team.

Blue-Collar Employees Without Email IDs
Since a significant portion of their workforce consists of blue-collar employees who may not have individual email IDs, they would like to understand whether employees can be registered on the platform using only their mobile/phone number.

If this is supported, please explain how the registration and authentication process would work in such a scenario. Specifically:

How would employees be onboarded/registered using their phone number?
If an employee does not have an email ID, how would gift voucher details and other important communications be delivered to them?
Are there any limitations in this use case?
