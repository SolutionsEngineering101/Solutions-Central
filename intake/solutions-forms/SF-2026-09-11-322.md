# Solution Request #322 - Strand Life Sciences
**Date:** 2026-09-11  |  **Status:** Solution Given Closed  |  **Complexity:** Not Set

## Brief
Customization in award nomination workflow – restricting denomination selection to approvers

## Problem Description
The client wants the nominator to only submit the nomination and provide the citation, without having the option to select the award denomination.

The approvers should be responsible for reviewing and approving the nomination and selecting the appropriate award denomination.

Please confirm if the workflow can be configured to support this requirement. If this is not possible, please advise on any alternative configurations or approaches that can achieve a similar outcome.

## Solution Given
We can configure the workflow to hide the award denomination/value at the nomination stage, so that the nominator is only required to submit the nomination and provide the citation and is applicable to all awards as this is a company specific configuration.
However, due to the current system architecture, the award denomination cannot be introduced or selected specifically at the approval stage. Therefore, our recommended approach would be:
Nomination Stage: Hide the award value, allowing the nominator to submit only the nomination details and citation.
Approval Stage: Approvers can review and approve the nomination, but the award denomination will not be selectable within the approval workflow.
Post-Approval: Once the nomination is approved, the Client HR Admin can manually upload/allocate the appropriate points to the approved recipients based on the denomination decided by the approvers.
The points can be uploaded with the privacy setting configured as “Private”, ensuring that no additional feed post is generated for the points allocation.
This approach would allow the client to maintain the desired separation between nomination submission and award-value decision-making, while working within the current platform capabilities.
