# Solution Request #340 - BSES Yamuna Power Ltd.
**Date:** 2026-10-05  |  **Status:** Pending Update  |  **Complexity:** Not Set

## Brief
Hi Team,

Requesting your involvement in reviewing the attached BRD received from BSES Yamuna Power Ltd (Part of Reliance Group), followed by a discussion with the client team to assess how well we align with their requirements.

Context:

BSES Yamuna Power has ~18,000 employees in India and is evaluating a digital R&R platform.
We’ve already completed one broad platform demo covering our key features and functionalities.
They have now shared a detailed BRD covering their current RACE programme and award workflows and would like us to deep dive into the specifics of how these can be configured on Vantage Circle.

Request:
Please review the attached BRD and help us assess the requirements from a Solution Engineering perspective. Once reviewed, I’d also like to schedule a discussion with the client team to walk them through the solution fitment and address their questions.

Sharing the client’s email below for context:

Dear Piyush,

Based on our discussions, we have consolidated our key business, functional and process requirements into the attached Business Requirements Document (BRD).

We are sharing the BRD with your team to seek a detailed assessment of the proposed requirements and understand how your platform can support the envisaged RACE ecosystem. We request you to review the document and provide your inputs covering the following aspects:

Platform capabilities available to meet the stated business and functional requirements
Features that can be enabled through standard configuration, customisation or development
Gaps, limitations or requirements that may not be currently supported
Proposed solution architecture and process flow for key RACE requirements
Technical, operational or business dependencies/assumptions
Proposed implementation approach, key milestones and indicative timelines
Commercial implications, including any additional costs towards customisation, integrations or specific requirements

The assessment will help us evaluate the overall solution fitment, feasibility, level of customization required and the proposed implementation approach for digitalizing the RACE programme.

Request you to kindly review the attached BRD and share your detailed solution assessment and proposal. Based on the inputs received, we can subsequently connect to discuss the requirements in detail and take the engagement forward.

Please let me know once you’ve had a chance to review the BRD, along with a few suitable slots for the client discussion this week.
