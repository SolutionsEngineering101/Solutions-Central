## Subject
Solution Request : 337337 : Reliance Brands Gift Cards Hosting

## Brief
Hi Team,

We’ve received a go-ahead from the Reliance Brands CEO, subject to us fulfilling the below requirements. Before we move into contracting, I’d like to get the Solutions Team involved to align on the workflows and ensure we set the right expectations with the client:

1. Competitor Gift Cards: No RBL competitor gift cards will be hosted on the platform. RBL will decide which gift cards can be hosted and will revisit the list every 3–6 months.
2. Reliance/RBL Gift Cards: Their own/Luxury gift cards will be hosted on the platform at no additional charge. RBL will share the employee list and denomination against which their team will distribute the vouchers.

To ensure there is complete clarity on the workflow from both sides and avoid any discrepancies later, I’d request someone from the Solutions Team to join a discussion with the client.

Please suggest a suitable time during the week of 5th October. This will be an important step before we proceed with contracting.
