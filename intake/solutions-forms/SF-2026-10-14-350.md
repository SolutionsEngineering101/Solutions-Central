Similar to the contact profile display shown in the attached reference, the notification should ideally support displaying either the company's logo or a program-specific image, enabling better program identification and a more personalized, branded communication experience for employees. Reason being that there may be instances where users overlook the email or mistakenly move it to their spam or scam folders, which could impact its visibility and reach.
