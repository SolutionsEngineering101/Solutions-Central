# Solution Request #341 - Vedanta Group
**Date:** 2026-10-06  |  **Status:** Pending Update  |  **Complexity:** Not Set

## Brief
Hi Team,

We’re in advanced discussion with Vedanta Group, comprising 13 different entities/lines of business, for a redemption platform covering ~17,000 employees in India, with an annual points spend of ~₹75 Cr.

Historically, Vedanta has processed incentives through payroll. They now want to move to a points-based platform to offer employees more redemption choices across their diverse workforce.

One key requirement is to integrate their internal incentive portal with Vantage Circle, so incentives awarded there can flow into Vantage Circle as points. There is also a requirement to handle different point-to-INR conversion ratios across entities. For example:

Iron Ore: 1,000 incentive points = ₹1,000
Aluminium: 1,000 incentive points = ₹2,000

It would be great to get your inputs on the best way to structure this workflow. Should the conversion happen within their internal portal before the points are pushed to Vantage Circle on a 1:1 basis, or is there a better way to manage entity-wise conversion within our platform?

Please share your thoughts on the most effective approach and any technical considerations we should factor in.
