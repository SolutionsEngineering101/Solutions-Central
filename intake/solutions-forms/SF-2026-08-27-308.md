# Solution Request #308 - ICICI Lombard
**Date:** 2026-08-27  |  **Status:** Open  |  **Complexity:** Medium Complexity

## Subject
ICICI Lombard Feasibility Assessment – Automated Points Allocation via CRM Integration (Company ID - 1804)

## Brief
We would like to understand the feasibility of integrating Microsoft Dynamics CRM with the I-Utsav platform through an API or any other suitable automation mechanism to give out automated points for successful lead generation.

## Problem Description
Hi Team,

ICICI Lombard is looking to automate the points allocation process for successful lead conversions and eliminate the current manual intervention.

Currently, the process works as follows:

1) Leads are generated through the Microsoft Teams bot.
2) Once a lead is qualified, it is tied and synced with the CRM (Microsoft Dynamics).
3) The business team works on the qualified leads and updates the lead/deal status in the CRM once the deal is successfully closed.
4) At present, the points for successful conversions are uploaded manually on the I-Utsav platform.


The client now wants to automate this process. The proposed workflow is that once a deal is marked as Closed/Successful in Microsoft Dynamics CRM, the relevant information should be automatically passed to the I-Utsav platform, and the predefined points should be automatically allocated to the user who successfully closed the lead.

We would like to understand the feasibility of integrating Microsoft Dynamics CRM with the I-Utsav platform through an API or any other suitable automation mechanism.

Could the Solutions team please assess and advise on the following:

1) Whether such an integration is technically feasible.
2) The recommended integration approach/API or automation mechanism.
3) The data points/fields that would need to be shared between Dynamics CRM and I-Utsav.
4) Any prerequisites or dependencies from the client's end.
5) Whether there are any limitations or considerations that we should communicate to the client.
6) An indicative effort and timeline, if feasible.

The objective is to ensure that the points are triggered and credited automatically to the appropriate user as soon as a deal is successfully closed in the CRM, thereby eliminating the need for manual point uploads.

Please review and let us know your thoughts on the feasibility and the recommended approach. 

Thanks.
