## Subject

## Brief
During the Sun Pharma QBR, the client suggested creating an automated “Did You Know?” communication series to improve awareness and adoption of underutilized recognition features.

Could we please explore whether we can automate recurring communications highlighting:

Service Anniversary Yearbooks – encourage managers and employees to add messages and make the yearbook more meaningful.
Non-monetary Recognition– promote the use of non monetary as an easy way to appreciate employees without using monetary budget.
Recognition Best Practices – share simple guidance and reminders on how managers and leaders can effectively use the recognition program.

The objective is to make these communications recurring and automated rather than relying on ad-hoc manual promotion.
