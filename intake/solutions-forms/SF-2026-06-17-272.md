# Solution Request #272 - Ultratech Wires and Cables
**Date:** 2026-06-17  |  **Status:** Solution Given Closed  |  **Complexity:** Not Set

## Subject
Solution Request : 272272 : Ultratech Wires & Cables Publish Award functionality

## Brief
Ultratech Wires & Cables is currently under implementation, and they have requested a Publish feature for Standard and Panel Awards.

What this feature entails:

After the award is approved in the frontend by the final approver or winner selector, they would have a Publish option

By clicking this button only, it will:

Publish the feed

Trigger the email

Award the points

Reason for the request:
Ultratech wants to park the final winners to be announced together on a particular day as an event within the organisation. This gives them control over when to publicly announce the winners rather than having them published immediately upon approval.

## Solution Given
As an alternative to developing a dedicated Publish feature, we recommend leveraging the existing approval workflow by introducing an additional final approval level in the award approval process.
Nominations would proceed through the configured approval stages as usual.
After the final business approval is completed, the nomination would move to a newly configured final approval stage (lets say if it is a 2 level approval award, it should have a 3rd level approver)
This final 3rd level approver can be designated as the Program Driver or any authorized administrator responsible for the announcement.
The award would remain pending until this final approval is provided.
Once the Program Driver approves:
Points will be awarded.
Recognition feed will be published.
Email notifications will be triggered.
The award will become visible across the platform.
